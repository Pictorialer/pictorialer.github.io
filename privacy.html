Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This policy explains how the Pictorialer app ("the app"), provided by 화보가 (Pictorialer) ("we"), handles your information.

1. Summary

  • No sign-up, no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking.
  • We never collect your name, email, phone number, photos, or precise location.
  • To improve the app, we collect anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase (section 6). We do not collect usage statistics from users in the EU, EEA, UK, or Switzerland (section 6.1).
  • The two phones talk to each other directly over Bluetooth. Every feature works without an internet connection; only buying or restoring Live View Remote needs one. Statistics are sent later, when the phone is online.

3. Photos and the live view

Photos are saved only on the camera phone. The live view image is sent only to the paired remote phone over Bluetooth, is shown on screen, and is not saved. Photos and live view images are never uploaded to the internet.

4. Information stored on your phone

The app saves the following only on your own phone: your language setting, whether you have bought Live View Remote, and the country of your App Store or Google Play account (used only to decide whether usage statistics may be collected, section 6.1). This information is not sent to us and is deleted when you delete the app.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never receive your name, card number, or other payment details. To unlock and lock Live View Remote correctly, the app checks your purchase status directly with Apple or Google from your phone: whether you have bought it, whether a payment is still pending, and whether it was refunded. A refunded purchase locks Live View Remote again. The app also reads the country of your store account. This information is not sent to us.

6. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google LLC, to understand how the app is used, find problems, and improve it.

What is collected
  • How the app is used: app opens, which buttons and screens are used (for example Simple or Live View Remote, shutter presses, timer settings, camera flips, language changes), whether the two phones connected and the reason if they did not, whether the purchase popup was shown, whether a purchase was made or restored (including its price and currency), and how many photos were taken (never the photos themselves)

  • A random app ID created when the app is installed (changes if you reinstall the app)
